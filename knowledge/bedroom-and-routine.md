A quiet, relaxing and cool bedroom supports sleep. A short, calming routine before bed, the same most nights,
signals that the day is ending: dimming lights, reading, stretching or a shower are common choices.

## Large meals late
Large meals right before bed can make it harder to sleep; if you are hungry late in the evening, a light
snack is the usual advice.
