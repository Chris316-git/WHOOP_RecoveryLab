---
id: caffeine-timing
title: Caffeine timing and sleep
topic: sleep
source: Drake et al., Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine (2013), via AASM
url: https://aasm.org/late-afternoon-and-early-evening-caffeine-can-disrupt-sleep-at-night/
---
In a laboratory study of 12 healthy sleepers, 400 mg of caffeine (roughly two to three cups of coffee) taken
at bedtime, 3 hours before or 6 hours before bed disrupted sleep in every case. Taken 6 hours before bed it
still reduced measured total sleep by more than an hour, and participants did not notice the difference
themselves. The authors suggested avoiding caffeine after 5 p.m.; sleep experts more generally advise avoiding
it in the afternoon and evening.
