Waking up at the same time every day, weekends and holidays included, is one of the core healthy sleep
habits. Choose a bedtime that leaves room for seven to eight hours of sleep, and go to bed when you feel
sleepy rather than lying awake.

## Shifting bedtime earlier
Moving a bedtime earlier works best in small steps, for example 15 to 20 minutes at a time, keeping the
wake-up time fixed. A large jump tends to mean lying awake, which makes the new time harder to keep.
