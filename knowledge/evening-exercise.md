title: Exercise in the evening and sleep
topic: training
source: Stutz et al., Sports Medicine (2019), systematic review and meta-analysis
url: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30374942/
---
A meta-analysis of 23 studies in healthy adults found that a single evening exercise session does not harm
sleep overall; if anything, slow-wave sleep increased slightly. The exception the authors flagged, tentatively,
was vigorous exercise that ends within about an hour of bedtime, which may delay sleep onset and reduce sleep
time and efficiency.

## In practice
Moderate exercise in the evening is generally fine. If a session is hard, finishing it earlier leaves time to
wind down before bed.
