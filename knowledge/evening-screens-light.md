Sleep experts advise turning off electronic devices at least 30 minutes before bed and limiting bright light
in the evening. Dimmer, calmer evenings make it easier to fall asleep at the intended time.

## When sleep does not come
If you cannot fall asleep after about 20 minutes, get up and do something quiet in low light, away from
screens, and go back to bed when sleepy. Staying in bed frustrated tends to make the next night harder.
