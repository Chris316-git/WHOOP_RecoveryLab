Heart rate variability changes a lot from one morning to the next with sleep, stress, illness, travel and
training. Sports scientists who monitor athletes therefore recommend looking at a rolling average over about a
week and at how much it varies, compared with the person's own baseline, rather than reacting to a single
reading. Values differ widely between people, so comparisons with other people mean little.
