A hard session is not suggested when the data argues against it: a 7-day HRV below your normal range, two or
more hard days in a row, a 7-day load more than 1.1 times the 4-week average, a weekly sleep shortfall of two
hours or more, or a recovery score that is not in the green zone. These are conservative rules of thumb written in code; the AI cannot override them. They are
not medical advice, and how you feel still matters.
