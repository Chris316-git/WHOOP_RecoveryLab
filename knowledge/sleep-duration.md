The American Academy of Sleep Medicine and the Sleep Research Society recommend that adults aged 18 to 60
sleep seven or more hours per night on a regular basis. Regularly sleeping six hours or less is considered
too little to sustain health and safety.

## Longer sleep and sleep debt
No upper limit was set. Sleeping more than nine hours can be appropriate for young adults, for people
recovering from sleep debt and for people who are ill. The panel also stressed that timing, regularity and
quality matter, not only the number of hours. Anyone who suspects a sleep disorder should see a clinician.
