title: Training load and sudden spikes

The acute:chronic workload ratio compares the last week of training with the average of the last four weeks.
Work in team sports suggested that sharp spikes in load, ratios well above about 1.3 to 1.5, went along with
higher injury risk, while building load gradually was protective. A ratio around 0.8 to 1.3 was described as a
reasonable range.

## Caveats
The ratio is a rule of thumb from sports science, not a law, and later work has criticised how it is
calculated and how strongly it predicts injury. Recovery Lab uses it only as one signal for when to keep a
day light.
